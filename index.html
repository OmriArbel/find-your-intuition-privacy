Privacy Policy

Find Your Intuition, Android app (com.findyourintuition)
Developer: Omri Arbel · Effective 29 October 2026

In short: Find Your Intuition does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone, and you can delete it at any time.

Who we are

Find Your Intuition is developed by Omri Arbel ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information. Questions can be sent to balanceboardgame@gmail.com.

Information the app stores on your device

To work, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • Your decisions: the two options you write, the result of the random draw, and the timing you chose (when the result becomes available and when to remind you).
  • Your notes: any optional note you write after a decision.
  • Timestamps for each step (created, revealed, completed) and the time zone in use when you created the decision, so reminders stay correct if you travel.
  • Simple preferences: whether you have seen the introduction, the "Reduce motion" setting, whether the app has already asked for notification permission, and which decisions have already shown their reveal animation.

What we collect and share

Nothing. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send information anywhere. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting service and no third-party code that collects data. We never receive your decisions or notes, and we do not sell or share any information with anyone.

How your information is protected

The data stays in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps and protects with your device's own encryption and screen lock.

Retention and deletion

  • Information is kept on your phone until you delete it.
  • You can delete any single decision, including its note, from inside the app.
  • To delete everything, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

Because we hold no copy of your information, there is nothing stored with us to request or delete.

Backups and device transfers

Your decisions and preferences are excluded from cloud backups. If you use Android's direct phone-to-phone transfer when setting up a new device, they may be copied to that device as part of the transfer, which you start and control.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses them only to tell you that a decision you set for later is ready. Notifications are created on your phone; nothing is sent over the network. They never show the result, and on the lock screen they do not show your options.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to tell you when a decision is ready. Optional; the app works without it.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to restore pending reminders after your phone restarts.

Services outside the app

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information about your device and app installation under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive personal data from this. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages; when you open it, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Since we hold none, all your information is already under your control on your device. You are welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If the app ever begins to collect data, the policy will be updated before that change is released.
